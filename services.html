One Team From Design to Launch

Many businesses in Sri Lanka end up juggling a designer, a separate developer, a hosting company and a marketing agency. When something goes wrong, nobody takes responsibility. We bring web design, web development, hosting, maintenance and SEO together, so your website is planned, built and supported by one accountable team.

You get one point of contact, consistent quality and a website that keeps working long after launch day.

Custom Web Development, Not Just Templates

Templates can be a quick start, but they often load slowly, look like hundreds of other sites and are hard to extend. We design every website around your brand and write clean, efficient code. For larger projects we build custom features with modern frameworks such as Laravel and React, so your website can grow with your business.

Platforms We Recommend

WordPress is ideal for business websites and blogs that you want to update yourself. WooCommerce and Shopify work well for online stores of most sizes. For booking systems, portals, POS and inventory tools, we build custom web applications with Laravel, React and Node.js. We will always recommend the platform that fits your budget and goals, not the one that is easiest for us.

FAQ

Web Development Services FAQs

Common questions about our web design and web development services in Sri Lanka.

Which web development services do you offer in Sri Lanka?

We offer website design, e-commerce website development, custom web applications, UI/UX design, SEO and digital marketing, and managed hosting and maintenance for businesses across Sri Lanka.

Do you use WordPress or custom code?

Both. We use WordPress, WooCommerce or Shopify when you want a site that is easy to update yourself, and custom development with Laravel, React or Node.js for booking systems, portals and other web applications.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We can redesign an old or slow website, keep your existing content and Google rankings where possible, and move it to faster, more secure hosting.

Do you provide SEO after the website is launched?

Every website includes on-page SEO foundations. For ongoing growth we offer monthly SEO and digital marketing with content, Google Business Profile optimisation, link building and clear ranking and traffic reports.

Can you integrate payment gateways and other systems?

Yes. We integrate Sri Lankan and international payment gateways, SMS and email notifications, WhatsApp, courier services, accounting tools and other third-party APIs.

Do you work with businesses outside Sri Lanka?

Yes. Alongside clients across Sri Lanka, we build websites for businesses overseas, including clients in the UAE, and work fully online.
