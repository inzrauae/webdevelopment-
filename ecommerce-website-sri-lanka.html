E-Commerce Website in Sri Lanka

Selling online in Sri Lanka keeps growing, from fashion and electronics to groceries and gifts. This guide explains what a successful Sri Lankan online store needs: payment options, delivery, product pages, marketing and costs.

Plan Your Online Store

Before building, decide what you will sell, how many products you have, who your customers are and how you will deliver. List your categories, prepare product photos and descriptions, and decide your delivery charges. Good planning makes the build faster and the store easier to use.

Payment Options for Sri Lankan Customers

Customers buy more when they can pay the way they prefer. Most Sri Lankan online stores offer a mix of:

  • Card payments through a local payment gateway or a bank's internet payment gateway.
  • Bank transfer, where the customer uploads a payment slip.
  • Cash on delivery (COD), which many first-time buyers still prefer.
  • International payments if you sell to customers overseas.

Payment gateways need your business registration and bank details. Start the application early, as approval can take some time.

Delivery and Shipping

Most stores work with local courier services for island-wide delivery. Set clear delivery charges, for example a lower rate inside Colombo and a different rate for other districts, and show estimated delivery times on product and checkout pages. Unclear delivery costs are one of the main reasons customers abandon their cart.

How to Write Product Pages That Sell

  1. Use a clear product name with the main keyword people search for.
  2. Show several photos from different angles, plus a photo in use.
  3. List key details: size, material, warranty and what is in the box.
  4. Show the price, delivery charge and delivery time clearly.
  5. Add an "Order on WhatsApp" button for customers who prefer to chat.

Which Platform Should You Use?

For most small and medium stores, WooCommerce on WordPress is flexible and affordable. Larger stores with many products, complex stock management or integrations with POS and accounting systems may need a custom build. Compare both in our WordPress vs custom website guide. If you also sell in a physical shop, a connected POS and inventory system keeps online and in-store stock in sync.

Getting Your First Orders

  • Optimise product and category pages for Google searches.
  • Share products on Facebook, Instagram and TikTok with links to your store.
  • Run small, targeted ads to your best-selling products.
  • Encourage reviews and repeat orders with discount codes.

Can I accept card payments on my website in Sri Lanka?

Yes. You can connect a local payment gateway or your bank's internet payment gateway to accept Visa and Mastercard. You will need business registration and bank details to apply.

Should I offer cash on delivery?

In Sri Lanka, cash on delivery is still popular, especially with first-time customers. Offering it alongside card and bank transfer usually increases orders.
