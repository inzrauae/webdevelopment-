What Affects the Cost of a Website?

  • Number of pages: more pages mean more design, content and testing time.
  • Custom design vs template: a design made for your brand takes more work than a ready-made theme.
  • Features: online payments, booking systems, multi-language content, member logins and integrations add cost (see our e-commerce and web development guides).
  • Content: whether you supply text and photos, or need copywriting and photography.
  • E-commerce size: the number of products, categories and delivery options.
  • SEO and marketing: basic on-page SEO is included; ongoing SEO campaigns are extra.
  • Deadline: urgent projects may need extra resources.

Is a Cheap Website Worth It?

Very cheap websites are often built from a basic template with little care for speed, SEO or mobile design. They may look acceptable at first, but they rarely bring in enquiries, and you may need to pay again for a proper website later.

On the other hand, a higher price does not always mean better quality. The best value comes from a company that shows live work, explains exactly what is included and builds the website around your customers. Read our guide on how to choose a web design company in Sri Lanka before you decide.

How to Save Money on Your Website

  1. Prepare your text, logo and photos before the project starts.
  2. Start with the pages you really need, and add more later.
  3. Choose the right platform for your needs (read WordPress vs custom website).
  4. Choose a package with a CMS so you can update content yourself.
  5. Collect 3 to 5 example websites you like to speed up design decisions.
  6. Give clear, combined feedback in one message instead of many small changes.
  7. Ask what is included up front to avoid paying for extras later.

Think of It as an Investment

A good website works for your business 24 hours a day. If it brings in even a few extra customers each month, it usually pays for itself quickly. That is why it is worth choosing a website that is fast, mobile-friendly and built to appear on Google, rather than simply the cheapest option.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a website cost in Sri Lanka?

With Web Development Sri Lanka, a starter business website costs LKR 20,000, a business website with a CMS and blog costs LKR 30,000, and an e-commerce website costs LKR 39,000. Custom web applications are quoted based on the features you need.

What is included in the website price?

All our packages include a responsive design, on-page SEO setup, free SSL, first-year hosting and support after launch. The number of pages and features depends on the package.

Are there yearly costs after the website is built?

Yes. Expect yearly domain renewal and hosting from the second year, plus optional maintenance. Online stores may also pay transaction fees to their payment provider.

How long does it take to build a website?

A starter website usually takes 1 to 2 weeks, a business website 3 to 4 weeks and an e-commerce website 4 to 6 weeks, depending on how quickly content and feedback are provided.
