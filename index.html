Buyer's guide

How to Choose the Right Web Design Company in Sri Lanka

Five quick checks that help you compare web design quotes and avoid costly surprises later.

  1. Look at real, live websitesOpen the portfolio sites on your own phone. Do they load quickly, look modern and work smoothly? Screenshots alone can hide problems.
  2. Ask what is includedCheck pages, hosting, SSL, domain, SEO setup, revisions and support period, so you can compare quotes fairly.
  3. Make sure you own itYour domain, content and design should belong to you once the project is paid for, with full admin access.
  4. Check SEO is built inTitles, meta descriptions, headings, alt text, speed and a sitemap should be standard, not an expensive extra.
  5. Ask about supportFind out who updates the site, how quickly they respond and what ongoing maintenance costs.

FAQ

Web Design Sri Lanka FAQs

Answers to the questions Sri Lankan businesses ask us most about getting a new website.

How much does a website cost in Sri Lanka?

With Web Development Sri Lanka, a starter business website starts from LKR 20,000, a custom business website with a CMS from LKR 30,000 and an e-commerce website with online payments from LKR 39,000. Custom web applications are quoted per project after a free consultation.

How long does it take to design and develop a website?

A starter website is usually ready in 1–2 weeks, a business website in 3–4 weeks and an online store in 4–6 weeks. Timelines depend on the number of pages, features and how quickly content is provided.

What is the difference between web design and web development?

Web design is how your website looks and feels: layout, colours, typography and user experience. Web development is the code that makes it work: pages, forms, online payments, databases and admin panels. We handle both, so you deal with one team from start to finish.

Do you build e-commerce websites with online payments in Sri Lanka?

Yes. We build online stores that accept card payments through Sri Lankan payment gateways, along with bank transfer and cash on delivery. They include product, stock, order and delivery management.

Will my website be mobile-friendly and SEO-ready?

Every website we build is fully responsive and includes on-page SEO: keyword-focused titles and meta descriptions, a proper heading structure, structured data, an XML sitemap, fast loading and Google Search Console setup.

Do you provide web design services outside Colombo?

Yes. We work with businesses across Sri Lanka, including Gampaha, Kandy, Galle, Matara, Kurunegala, Jaffna, Batticaloa and Trincomalee. Everything can be done online, by phone or over WhatsApp.

Can I update my website myself after it is launched?

Yes. We build on an easy-to-use content management system and show you how to edit text, images, products and blog posts. If you prefer, we can make updates for you under a maintenance plan.

Do you provide hosting, a .lk domain and maintenance?

Every package includes free SSL and first-year hosting. We can register a .lk or .com domain for you or connect one you already own, and we offer affordable monthly maintenance covering backups, security updates and content changes.
